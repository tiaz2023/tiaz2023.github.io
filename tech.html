Agentic Creation for 3D, 4D & Games (cooking 🔧)

Featured project · Roblox · 2026

Roblox Reality

Video models as an AI renderer for interactive 3D worlds.

The game engine provides world structure and game logic; a video model transforms its output into richer visuals. Creator control guides the appearance, style, and freedom of the generative renderer.

Generating realistic urban crowd videos from coarse 3D simulations, with control over scene layout, camera motion, human trajectories, and appearance.

DELTA: Dense Efficient Long-range 3D Tracking for any video (ICLR 2025)
